PLATFORM FEATURES

Useful tools for feed and silage decisions.

Record samples, screen them against reference ranges for each feed type, and keep useful information together.

🌱

Feed and silage samples

Record pH, moisture, temperature, smell and mold for corn silage, haylage, hay, green fodder and more.

✓

Transparent scoring

A 0–100 screening score with a per-check breakdown, so you can see exactly why a sample was flagged.

↻

Feed-type reference ranges

Silage is judged as fermented feed and hay as dry feed, so well-made silage is not penalised for a low pH.

📁

History and export

Filter your samples by feed type or result, edit records, and download everything as a CSV file.

🔒

Private by default

Each farmer sees only their own samples. Other accounts cannot open your records.

⚠

Honest limits

This is an illustrative screening tool. It does not replace laboratory analysis or professional advice.
